# Reference notes

Studied frame by frame on 2026-10-06 (videos from recent.design, not stored here). The owner accepted this mapping as the starting point. Motion details may still change, so every value below is a parameter in the prototype.

| Reference | What happens | What AZUR takes from it |
|---|---|---|
| Grokipedia book covers | A shelf of spines. Hover lifts a spine slightly. Click pulls the book out and turns its cover to the viewer while neighbours slide aside. A small line underneath: number, title, reads, "Read the article →", "Put back". | The core jersey interaction. Hover: the jersey lifts on its hanger. Click: it swivels toward the viewer, neighbours give way. One quiet line underneath: "03 Berlin 030 Trikot · 64,99 € · Ansehen → · Zurückhängen". |
| Digital wardrobe app | Garments on hangers in horizontal rows. Swiping scrolls the row, garments swing on their hangers with momentum and settle. | Hanger swing physics (pendulum with damping) when the rail moves. On mobile you swipe along the rail. |
| Holographic email confirmation | A ticket-like card tilts with the cursor, an iridescent sheen slides across it, soft coloured light behind. | The confirmation after signing up at the covered "Nächster Drop" jersey, restrained and in the Azur palette. |
| Gradient AI feature cards | 3D carousel of glowing gradient cards with a coloured aura beneath. | Only the soft light pool under the selected jersey. No carousel. |

Owner feedback on interaction: overlapping jerseys are fine. The visitor hovers through them; details appear only on click, as a small info with a link to the product page.
